Open Mic Night

Category: Arts & Performance

Date & Time: Friday, September 25 · 7:00–9:30 PM

Location: Student Union Theater, Phoenix University

Join fellow students for an evening of music, spoken word, comedy, and storytelling. No experience is required—just bring your voice, instrument, or creative spirit.

Event Overview

Open Mic Night is hosted by the Phoenix Performing Arts Collective and is open to all students. The event is designed to be supportive and inclusive, giving performers of all levels a chance to share their work.

What to Expect

  • Short performance slots (3–5 minutes) for music, poetry, comedy, or storytelling.
  • Professional sound equipment and a student-run tech team.
  • Casual seating with light refreshments provided.

Schedule

  1. 6:30 PM: Performer check-in and sound checks.
  2. 7:00 PM: Opening remarks and first performances.
  3. 8:15 PM: Intermission and networking.
  4. 8:30 PM: Second set of performances.
  5. 9:30 PM: Closing and informal jam session.

Accessibility & Participation

The Student Union Theater is wheelchair accessible, with reserved seating near the front. ASL interpretation can be requested by emailing the Office of Student Engagement at least 72 hours before the event.

Performers may submit content warnings for sensitive material, which will be announced before their set. Audience members are encouraged to be respectful and supportive of all performers.
